Artist Interview

In their own
words.

Studio portrait
Ah-young Shin · Melbourne
I paint at the exact moment I should be asleep. That's the only rule.

When do you actually make the work?

Between midnight and whenever my eyes give up. I've tried painting in daylight — it just doesn't feel true.

What's "Wave Under the Blanket" actually about?

Being awake next to someone who's asleep. That specific kind of quiet.

About Us

A little
about us.

Started by one insomniac. Kept going by a small crew who still haven't fixed their sleep schedule.

Slept on it began as a habit — scrolling too late, saving whatever stopped the thumb. It's grown into a small, artist-run shelf for young Australian talent: no investors, no gallery politics, just work worth staying up for. Everything on this site is picked, priced, and answered for by an actual person, usually after midnight.
